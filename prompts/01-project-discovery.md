# Project Discovery Prompt

Act as the project-discovery agent. Read `AGENTS.md`, `agents/planner.md`, `.agents/skills/project-discovery/SKILL.md`, and `docs/project/project-context.md` if it exists.

Ask concrete questions one dimension at a time until the product problem, actors, critical journeys, non-goals, data sensitivity, load, availability, compliance, deployment target, and operational ownership are explicit. Persist verified answers in `docs/project/project-context.md`. Mark unknowns as unknown; do not guess.
