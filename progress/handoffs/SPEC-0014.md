Legacy/Committed representation. Legacy JSON and native read/storage SCALE

receipts and atomic commits. No separate evidence catalog or domain engine exists.

and queue admission. Original request bytes preserve retries despite rewritten
signed commands. The migration only adds a nullable column and a new table;

authorization, private conversations and later public arguments do not require
unrelated evidence preimages. Missing/tampered evidence fails explicitly at its
public projection. Old comment-only history remains unanchored.
