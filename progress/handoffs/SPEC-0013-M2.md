## Delivered candidate

- Compiled-in immutable bundles verify artifact, WASM and build-lock hashes.
  Runtime identity includes configuration sources and the complete locked graph.
- Version-2 checkpoints retain complete raw tries, including contract child
  storage, and a root/runtime/bundle identity plus corruption digest. Decoding
  bounds bytes/allocations/node counts/refcounts and reconstruction copy work; restore verifies hashes and
  exhaustively traverses top and child tries with a work budget. Version-1 proof
  snapshots are rejected; no provider ever stored them.
- One dedicated thread runs typed metered calls/instantiations. Admission uses a
  bounded eight-job queue; limits cover input/output, salt, gas and opaque shell
  envelopes. No unmetered execution closure or arbitrary WASM upload is exposed.
- Candidate SQLite state commits runtime, opaque complete shell envelope, exact
  outcome and revision in one transaction. `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` plus expected
  revision prevents stale competing commits. Each job reads current durable
  state. Rejected calls retain the original business checkpoint and persist the
  failure envelope. Storage/validation failure commits nothing. Read queries
  discard all effects, even if the requested method mutates.
- Private library diagnostics return revision, root, identity and checkpoint
  size. No HTTP route is added. Shutdown stops admission, completes current work
  and rejects queued requests; explicit async shutdown observes the thread join.
  Dropping the owner signals shutdown and the worker observes/logs its result.
  Requests cancelled before execution are skipped; cancellation after work starts
  cannot imply rollback and never triggers fallback/re-execution.

## Acceptance boundary

milestone 2 complete** or enable a sandbox provider. `ShellChanges` currently
contains test envelopes; actual provider nonce/receipt/event encoding and the
business ABI bridge are not connected. They must be integrated under the
resolved dependency policy and tested through the signed operation shell before
M2 promotion. The existing provider remains Rust-authoritative.
